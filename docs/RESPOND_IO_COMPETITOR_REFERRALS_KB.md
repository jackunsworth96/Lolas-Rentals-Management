# Lola's Rentals - Competitor Vehicle Referral Knowledge Base

Version: 1.0
Last updated: 2026-08-05

This KB is designed for Respond.io AI knowledge ingestion alongside the main Lola's Rentals KB.
Use it only when a customer wants a vehicle Lola's does not stock, asks for another rental business, or clearly rejects the Lola's alternative already offered.

---

## Referral Behaviour

Be helpful, brief, and neutral. Never speak negatively about another rental business.

Once a customer clearly says they want a different model or vehicle type, asks who else may have it, or declines the Lola's alternative:

- Stop trying to sell or re-offer the Honda Beat or TukTuk.
- Recommend the most relevant business below.
- Do not claim that another business has live availability, a specific unit, or a specific price. The customer must check with that business directly.
- Do not describe these businesses as verified partners or imply a formal partnership.
- Do not repeat Lola's pricing, Paw Card benefits, delivery options, or booking link in the same reply.
- Do not ask for pickup and return times unless the customer returns to discussing a Lola's vehicle.
- If no exact match is known, say the suggested business is worth asking rather than promising they stock the requested model.

A customer asking, "Do you know anyone with a Honda Click?" after being offered a Honda Beat is asking for a referral. Answer the referral request directly and do not offer the Honda Beat again.

## Recommended Businesses

### Renta Gao

- Near Lola's store.
- Has a selection of scooters and motorbikes.
- Best first suggestion for a different scooter or motorbike model, including a Honda Click enquiry when exact stock is unknown.

Suggested reply:
"Renta Gao is nearby and has a selection of scooters and motorbikes, so they're worth asking about a Honda Click. I can't confirm their live availability, though."

### Coco Cruisers

- Near Lola's store.
- Has Jimny cars.
- Has a selection of retro-looking scooters, including Yamaha Fazzio and Honda Giorno models.
- Best suggestion for cars, Jimnys, Yamaha Fazzios, Honda Giornos, or retro-style scooters.

Suggested reply:
"Coco Cruisers is nearby and has Jimny cars plus retro-style scooters such as the Yamaha Fazzio and Honda Giorno. It's worth checking their availability directly."

### Golden Bell

- Slightly farther away from Lola's store.
- Has a selection of scooters and motorbikes.
- Has good Google reviews.
- Another useful suggestion for a different scooter or motorbike model.

Suggested reply:
"Golden Bell is a little farther away, but they have a selection of scooters and motorbikes and good Google reviews. They're worth asking about the model you need."

### Emotion Siargao

- Suitable for customers looking for e-bikes.
- Best suggestion when the customer specifically asks for an e-bike or electric bike.

Suggested reply:
"For an e-bike, try Emotion Siargao. They're the most suitable option to check with directly."

## Choosing A Referral

- Different or specific scooter/motorbike: Renta Gao first; Golden Bell is another option.
- Honda Click: Renta Gao or Golden Bell, framed as businesses worth asking. Never promise that a Honda Click is available.
- Jimny or another car: Coco Cruisers.
- Yamaha Fazzio, Honda Giorno, or retro-style scooter: Coco Cruisers.
- E-bike or electric bike: Emotion Siargao.
- General request for other rental places with no vehicle preference: give a short list of two or three relevant options, not every business by default.

Example:

Customer: "Thanks, but I specifically want a Honda Click. Do you know anyone?"

Reply: "Renta Gao is nearby and has a selection of scooters and motorbikes, so they're worth asking. Golden Bell is another well-reviewed option a little farther away. I can't confirm either one's live Honda Click availability."

Do not follow that reply with another offer for a Honda Beat or TukTuk.
