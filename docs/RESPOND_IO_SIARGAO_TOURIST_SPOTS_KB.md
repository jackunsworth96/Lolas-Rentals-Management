# Siargao Tourist Spots and Visitor Guidance

Use this Knowledge Base when a customer asks about places to visit, sightseeing, beaches, caves, pools, island tours, DIY land tours, parking at attractions, or what to do around Siargao.

## Response Guidance

- Recommend places from this guide instead of saying there is no reliable information.
- Ask one short preference question only when needed, such as whether they prefer beaches, swimming, adventure, sightseeing, or a boat trip.
- Keep recommendations concise and mention the most useful timing, safety, access, or parking detail.
- When a customer shares a bad, stressful, or unfair experience, acknowledge it with empathy before giving advice.
- Do not invent opening hours, prices, tide conditions, restrictions, travel times, or local enforcement rules.
- Treat prices and travel times below as general guidance because they may change.

## General Parking Guidance

The general rule is to park more than 2 meters away from the main road. Use clearly permitted parking, keep the vehicle fully off the traffic lane, and follow official signs or instructions from the attraction or accommodation.

Do not claim to know every restricted parking spot. If someone alleges a parking violation, advise the customer to ask for the official notice or posted rule before paying. If the customer describes being scammed or unfairly targeted, acknowledge how frustrating that must have been before sharing the parking guidance.

## DIY Siargao Land Tour

Coconut View Deck, Maasin River, Magpupungko Rock Pools, Tayangban Cave, and Alegria Beach can be combined into a good DIY land tour. Doot Beach is a closer beach option near General Luna and can be visited separately.

### Coconut View Deck

[Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Coconut+Plantation+View+Point,+R365%2BWRH,+Siargao+Circumferential+Rd,+Pilar,+Surigao+del+Norte/@11.1859168,123.7741168,5159m/data=!3m2!1e3!4b1!4m8!4m7!1m0!1m5!1m1!1s0x33040753d25f230f:0x69f6af07d07b34e8!2m2!1d126.0596118!2d9.8123234?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDcyMi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)

- One of Siargao's most photographed viewpoints.
- It is a roadside pull-off rather than an actual viewing deck.
- Overlooks coconut trees stretching toward the mountains.
- Free to visit and especially scenic around sunset.
- Local photographers may offer aerial-style "human drone" photos for a tip.
- It is beside a busy main road. Stay on the shoulder, away from traffic, and park more than 2 meters from the main road.

### Maasin River

[Google Maps](https://google.com/maps?vet=10CAAQoqAOahcKEwjQxPOLqu2VAxUAAAAAHQAAAAAQBQ..i&rlz=1C1VDKB_en-GBGB1005GB1005&sca_esv=599ad5a4315ad03b&pvq=Cg0vZy8xMWd2MHAzaHI4IhIKDG1hYXNpbiByaXZlchACGAM&lqi=ChRtYWFzaW4gcml2ZXIgc2lhcmdhb0jo_caK6K6AgAhaIBAAEAEYABgBGAIiFG1hYXNpbiByaXZlciBzaWFyZ2FvkgESdG91cmlzdF9hdHRyYWN0aW9u&fvr=1&cs=1&um=1&ie=UTF-8&fb=1&gl=ph&sa=X&geocode=Ka_6jlDmBwQzMRhBs2rBwqGD&daddr=R3C4%2BHFW,+Siargao+Circumferential+Rd,+Pilar,+Surigao+del+Norte)

- Guided canoe tours travel through mangroves and palm forest.
- Tours can end at the Secret Lagoon, a clear, cold spring suitable for swimming.
- The former coconut-tree rope swing was replaced by a jumping platform after typhoon damage.
- Around 2 minutes from Coconut View Deck.
- Roughly a 20-minute motorbike ride from General Luna on the main road toward Magpupungko Rock Pools.

### Magpupungko Rock Pools

[Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Magpupungko+Tidal+Pool,+Pilar,+Surigao+del+Norte/@11.1859168,123.7741168,5159m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m8!4m7!1m0!1m5!1m1!1s0x3304090c9e2779d9:0x6b158a5816ad122a!2m2!1d126.1199161!2d9.875508?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDcyMi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)

- Tidal rock pools appear at low tide.
- The main pool has a central rock commonly used for jumping.
- Best for swimming and exploring at low tide.
- At high tide the pools can be dangerous; the adjacent beach is the better place to relax.
- Around 45 minutes by motorbike from General Luna.
- General price guidance: ₱60 per person entry plus ₱20 per motorbike for parking.
- Use the official main parking area. Be cautious if someone away from the official entrance flags visitors down and claims the parking is full.

### Tayangban Cave Pool

[Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Tayangban+Cave+Pool,+V367%2BG55,+Siargao+Circumferential+Rd,+Pilar,+Surigao+del+Norte/@11.1859168,123.7741168,5159m/data=!3m2!1e3!4b1!4m8!4m7!1m0!1m5!1m1!1s0x33040641428b3beb:0xdba8e40a5e7cffac!2m2!1d126.0629932!2d9.861264?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDcyMi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)

- A roughly 240-metre wet cave trek through dark passageways using a headlamp.
- The trek emerges at a natural pool with a jumping platform and rope swing.
- Water depth depends on the tide; high tide can make sections deeper and harder to pass.
- Water shoes are recommended.
- Leave phones behind unless they are properly waterproofed.

### Alegria Beach

[Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Alegria+Beach,+333C%2BVC7,+Alegria,+Santa+Monica+(Sapao),+Surigao+del+Norte/@11.1859168,123.7741168,5159m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m8!4m7!1m0!1m5!1m1!1s0x330419718c6e68e3:0x20f7fdd460e31d01!2m2!1d126.0710457!2d10.054659?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDcyMi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)

- A scenic drive of roughly 1.5 hours north from General Luna.
- The route passes Tayangban Cave, Magpupungko Rock Pools, San Isidro, and TakTak Falls, Siargao's only waterfall.
- Known for white sand, shallow swimmable water, coconut palms, and fewer tourists.
- Development is minimal, with a few basic cottages and little commercial activity.

### Doot Beach

[Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir//Doot+Beach/@9.753995,126.0546201,10021m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m17!1m7!3m6!1s0x3303f70ddce43237:0x7a6fafb38d1b6abc!2sDoot+Beach!8m2!3d9.7565791!4d126.1160469!16s%2Fg%2F12hk7nvyz!4m8!1m0!1m5!1m1!1s0x3303f70ddce43237:0x7a6fafb38d1b6abc!2m2!1d126.1160469!2d9.7565791!3e0?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDcyOS4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)

- A convenient beach-day option not far from General Luna.
- Visitors can hire a paddleboard or kayak and explore the mangroves.

## Island and Boat Trips

### Tri-Island Tour

- Boat trip visiting Naked Island, Daku Island, and Guyam Island.
- Naked Island is a bare sandbar with no shade.
- Daku Island is a common lunch stop, with fresh seafood often served in a hut.
- Guyam Island is known for its tree cover.
- Good for swimming, lunch, and photos.

### Mamon Island

- A more remote and less touristy island experience.
- Roughly a two-hour boat ride.
- Clear water makes it a strong snorkelling option.
- The island has no electricity supply.
- A small number of local families live there, so visitors should be respectful.

### Sohoton Cove

- A full-day trip to Bucas Grande Island in Socorro.
- Known for turquoise water, limestone cliffs, and hidden lagoons.
- Highlights can include Hagukan or the "snoring cave," a stingless-jellyfish sanctuary, kayaking on Tiktikan Lake, and cliff jumps up to roughly 30 feet.
- This is the longest and most intensive boat trip listed in this guide.

## Quick Recommendation Guide

- Scenic viewpoint and photos: Coconut View Deck.
- River and mangroves: Maasin River.
- Low-tide swimming and rock pools: Magpupungko.
- Adventure and cave trek: Tayangban Cave Pool.
- Quiet beach and scenic ride: Alegria Beach.
- Nearby beach and paddling: Doot Beach.
- Classic first-time island hopping: Tri-Island Tour.
- Remote snorkelling trip: Mamon Island.
- Full-day lagoons and caves: Sohoton Cove.

## Example Response Style

If a customer asks for good places to visit:

"Definitely! For a DIY land tour, Coconut View Deck, Maasin River, Magpupungko Rock Pools, and Tayangban Cave work well together. If you prefer a relaxed beach day, try Alegria or the closer Doot Beach. Are you more interested in swimming, sightseeing, or adventure?"

If a customer describes a bad parking experience:

"I'm sorry you had to deal with that. That sounds really frustrating. The general rule is to park more than 2 meters away from the main road, and it is safest to use clearly permitted parking or somewhere the attraction confirms is okay."
